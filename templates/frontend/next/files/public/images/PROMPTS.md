# Replacement image prompts

The current `.jpeg` files are demo photography provided for this template.
Replace them if the app is not a studio, or keep them as the starting art
direction. Keep the filenames, or update the image paths in
`src/app/page.tsx`.

The direction is documentary, warm, and rooted: Pacific Northwest overcast
light, real people and hands, cream and charcoal clothing, with small teal and
coral details. Avoid staged office stock, generic technology imagery, purple
gradients, and text inside the image.

## `hero.jpeg`

> Editorial documentary photograph of a diverse group of creative collaborators
> laughing together outside a white brick studio in Tacoma, Washington. Overcast
> Pacific Northwest light, candid 35mm film grain, natural skin tones, cream and
> charcoal clothing with subtle teal and coral details. Wide composition with
> breathing room for a large wordmark. No text, logos, signs, or watermarks.

## `brand.jpeg`

> Close-up editorial still life of hands arranging printed brand materials on a
> warm wooden table: cream paper, charcoal type, teal ink, and a coral color
> swatch. Soft window light, shallow depth of field, tactile paper texture. No
> readable brand names, text, logos, or watermarks.

## `product.jpeg`

> Over-the-shoulder documentary photograph of a designer reviewing a calm
> cream-and-charcoal digital product interface on a laptop in a warm studio.
> Natural daylight, candid posture, real hands, subtle teal and coral interface
> accents. No readable text, logos, or watermarks.

## `featured.jpeg`

> Evening community workshop in a warm studio: a small group gathered around a
> table, people talking and sketching, string lights and soft pools of light.
> Candid editorial photography, cinematic but natural, charcoal shadows with
> cream highlights. No text, logos, or watermarks.
