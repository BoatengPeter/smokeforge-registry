# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, React, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui. Existing code is the
starting point; do not replace the stack without explicit approval.

## Users

Smoke Forge is the independent practice of Boateng Peter, a software engineer
building web products, developer tools, and the systems around them. The
audience is prospective collaborators, clients, and other developers evaluating
his work and point of view.

## Product Purpose

Give Smoke Forge a warm, editorial home for its work, services, and point of
view. Success means a visitor understands what he builds, sees evidence of his
craft, and knows how to start a conversation.

## Positioning

A personal developer presence rather than a generic SaaS landing page or a
project list. The site should feel made by a person with a craft, a point of
view, and an interest in the people using the work.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from referrals, social links, and search, often on a phone.
They scan the first screen, look for recognizable work or values, and decide
within a few seconds whether to keep reading. The primary action is a
conversation, not a signup.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Editorial marketing landing page with anchored sections for work, services,
  and contact.
- Optional Postgres, Better Auth, and Zustand overlays may be present.
- The product name and description come from template values.
- Content is personalized for Smoke Forge and Boateng Peter; replace role,
  links, and contact details if they change.
- Do not invent clients, testimonials, awards, metrics, or project outcomes.

## Brand Commitments

- Warm, human, and direct.
- Developer-first language; no corporate abstraction or growth-hack claims.
- Documentary imagery over abstract stock technology imagery.
- The visual system is documented in `DESIGN.md` and must be preserved unless
  the user explicitly asks for a new direction.

## Evidence on Hand

No project case studies, testimonials, press, or outcomes are included. The
landing page is a designed starting point, not a project list. Add evidence
only when it is real.

## Product Principles

1. **People before polish.** Show the humans and the work; effects serve them.
2. **Rooted, not regionalist.** Make the local point of view feel generous.
3. **One clear next step.** Every section makes the path to a conversation easier.
4. **Calm confidence.** Space, scale, and edit rather than noise.
5. **Adapt everywhere.** The site must feel intentional at every screen size.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: readable contrast, visible focus, keyboard access, semantic
headings, touch targets of at least 44px, and reduced-motion support. Images
need meaningful alternative text unless they are purely decorative.
